The whole of Friday 27 November 2026 in a single picture. A giant golden globe balloon — the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade — sits GROUNDED by a huge storm, tethered low over a rain-soaked New York street with a big red GROUNDED stamp on its skin, while a crowd in bright rain ponchos gazes up at it in wonder beneath storm clouds, rain and lightning. The street itself carries the storm's toll: 500,000+ homes without power, hundreds of flights cancelled, 80 million people travelling for Thanksgiving. Below, a ticker runs the rest of the day: the Iran drone strike on Kuwait, the strikes in Gaza, the Bangkok refinery fire, Gukesh vs Sindarov's marathon Game 3, 300+ Russian troops killed in Mali, Trump's "fake" GDP probe, UK net migration at its lowest since 1997, and more.

Everything is drawn by `main.py` using only the Python standard library — its own 5x7 bitmap font, its own canvas and shape code, its own PNG encoder. No image assets, no network, no third-party packages. The output is a 1024x1024 PNG, and running the program twice produces identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
