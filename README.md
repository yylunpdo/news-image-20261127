One image that sums up the world's news of Friday 27 November 2026: a colossal golden globe balloon — the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, GROUNDED by a huge storm — tethered low over a rain-lashed New York street, its skin stamped GROUNDED, while ordinary people in bright rain ponchos point up in awe under storm clouds, rain and lightning. The storm's toll is written on the street: 500,000+ homes without power, hundreds of flights cancelled, 80 million travelling for Thanksgiving. A ticker strip carries the day's headlines: the Iran drone strike on Kuwait, Israel's strikes in Gaza, the Bangkok refinery fire, Gukesh vs Sindarov's marathon Game 3, 300+ Russian troops killed in Mali, Trump's "fake" GDP probe, UK net migration at its lowest since 1997, and more.

Pure Python standard library: `main.py` draws everything from scratch (storm sky, balloon, continents, tether ropes, poncho crowd, text) and writes a 1024x1024 PNG. Deterministic — the same bytes on every run.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
